1 · The opportunity

Huge, growing, and leaking viewers.

Downloads are growing much faster than revenue, and almost nobody stays. Viewers try these apps and leave. In reviews they say why: surprise charges, broken ad unlocks and prices they can't predict. That is the gap Spool is designed into.

2 · What 16 Google Play apps show

Same red-and-gold look. Same complaints.

Live listings pulled on 9 Oct 2026, with the most-relevant reviews on each page. Two things stand out. Every leader dresses in red, maroon and gold. And the top reviews are almost all about money that behaves unpredictably.

Patterns worth keeping
Coming-soon rows with “Remind me” (ReelShort, NetShort) · daily, weekly and monthly rankings (DramaWave) · trope filters · episode selector, speed and clean-screen mode (PineDrama) · 240p data saver and dubbed picks (FreeReels) · offline downloads (ShortMax) · watch-time rewards · language-first browsing for India (QuickTV, Kuku TV).
Why Spool is chartreuse
Every leading app uses red, maroon and gold. Spool's interface is ink and cream with one “greenlight” accent. It stands out in the store, never competes with the warm key art, and stays clear of the indigo and teal used in your other prototypes. Operators can switch the accent to Ember, Iris, Rosé or Aqua from one setting.
3 · The product

Stories that hook you. Prices that don't.

Positioning: the short-drama app people can trust with their money, plus a creator layer where fans and filmmakers feed the catalogue. Short dramas lead every surface. Web series, films, trailers and creator videos share the same content model, wallet and paywall.

5 · Design language

Calm chrome. Loud stories.

6 · The prototypes

Every screen, one click away.

The mobile prototype puts the phone at the centre. Around it sit the journey, a screen map, a live event stream (analytics, ledger rows, API calls, pushes), what to try on this screen with the research behind it, and scenario controls for persona, region, wallet, pushes, accent and speed.

Start with a journeyPick “The paywall moment” in the Journey panel and press Next step: E7's cliffhanger, the Cliffhanger Call, then Fair Unlock.
Change the scenarioSwitch region to India to see free-with-ads mode, set the wallet to 20 coins to hit the top-up flow, or fire the win-back push.
Then open the studioThe same titles, creators and ledger power the admin panel. Change the accent in Settings › Branding and the app follows.
7 · What happens next

Decisions for the team.

Confirm the name and accent“Spool” and chartreuse are working choices. Both are a single config value (brand name, --accent).
Lock v1.0 scopeLive streaming, interactive branching and downloads for free users stay on the roadmap. Pilot Season can launch as voting-only first.
Pick vendorsSubtitles and AI dubbing (human QA required), ad mediation, and a storage/CDN default for buyers.
Write store-policy rules per countryWeb checkout links are an admin switch per region, off by default except where policy allows.
Prototype on real devicesValidate the vertical player's preload and the swipe-to-unlock moment in Flutter first. It's the riskiest piece.
