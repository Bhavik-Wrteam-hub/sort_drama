Glossary

Words you'll see everywhere

Spool has its own names for several features. Text in a chip, like Unlock with coins, appears in the app exactly as written.

Coins
The in-app currency. An episode usually costs 60 coins, about $0.59.
Paid and bonus coins
Paid coins are bought with money and never expire. Bonus coins are free (check-ins, ads, offers), expire after 7 days and are always spent first.
Unlock
Opening a locked episode. It stays unlocked on the account, on any phone.
Series Pass
Every episode of a series for one price, including future drops.
VIP
A weekly, monthly or yearly plan. Everything unlocked and never an ad.
Fair Unlock
The sheet on a locked episode, with every option priced in coins and money before a tap.
Ledger
A record of every coin in and out. Nothing is edited or deleted; corrections are new rows.
Spend guard
A daily spending limit (300 coins by default), like screen time for money.
Price lock
The per-episode price a viewer started a series with can't rise for them.
Cliffhanger Call
A prediction at the end of an episode, such as “Who sent Ava the text?” A correct call earns +5 bonus coins.
Pulse Bar
The bar under the player that shows where viewers reacted most. Tap a 😱 to jump to that moment.
Story Rewind
A 20-second recap for viewers coming back after a break, without spoilers.
Who's who
A character map that only shows the people you've already met.
Scene Search
Find a moment by describing it, like “the slap at the gala”.
Fan Link
A creator tags a drama in their video. Viewers get a Watch button and the creator earns a share.
Pilot Season
Creators post 2–3 episode pilots, viewers vote Want more, and at 10,000 votes the team considers a full season.
Trope
The kind of story: Revenge, Billionaire, Werewolf, Hidden identity and so on.
Drop
A new episode release, such as “daily at 8 PM”.
Promise Guard
Checks that what an ad promises (“the first 7 episodes free”) is true in the app before the campaign can run.
SLA
How quickly a review must be decided. Two hours here.

The paywall moment

When and how money comes in

The most important moment: the viewer is hooked and the next episode is locked. Take Maya Rao. She has watched E1–E7 of The Heiress Returns for free, and her wallet holds 290 paid + 20 bonus = 310 coins.

  1. 1
    End of E7: Cliffhanger Call

    The question: “Who sent Ava the text?” Celeste 46% · Ethan 33% · Victor 21%. The answer arrives in E8; a correct call earns +5 bonus coins. Skip is always there.

  2. 2
    E8 is locked: Fair Unlock

    Four ways in, each priced up front: 60 coins (≈ $0.59) · one ad, free · Series Pass 1,990 · VIP $5.99 a week. Unlock E8 · 60 coins starts E8 straight away.

  3. 3
    The wallet row

    A new row: “Unlocked · The Heiress Returns E8 · −60 · 20 bonus first, then 40 paid”, with the balance after it: 250 · 0.

How Maya's 60 coins were taken
Before290 paid + 20 bonus = 310
E8 price60
From bonus first−20 → bonus 0
Then from paid−40 → paid 250
After250 paid + 0 bonus = 250

Bonus coins expire after 7 days, so they go first and the coins Maya paid for last longer. Unlocking the same episode again never charges twice.

Two things always sit under the Fair Unlock options:

  • Auto-unlock is off by default. When on, the next episode opens without asking, but never past the daily limit.
  • Price locked at 60 for you. Maya started this series at 60 coins an episode, so a later price rise doesn't reach her.
  • Balance: paid and bonus coins, and when the bonus expires (16 Oct).

Other situations

Short of coins

Only 20 coins in the wallet, so the sheet selects Watch 1 ad on its own. Top up → “You need 40 more coins for E8” → 1,000 + 200 bonus ($9.99) → 1-tap buy in Google Play. The coins land and E8 unlocks itself; nobody is sent back.

India: free with ads

With the region set to India, the ad is preselected. Sindoor Ka Badla has 15 free episodes, then 30 coins (≈ ₹8.7) or one ad, up to 20 a day. If the ad fails, the episode unlocks anyway: “The ad didn't finish — you get the reward anyway.”

Daily limit reached

Maya's limit is 300 coins a day. With 260 spent and E8 costing 60, the unlock pauses and offers three choices: Raise today to 600 and unlock, Watch 1 ad instead, Not now. Nothing is charged without a decision.

VIP, done honestly

$5.99 a week · $19.99 a month · $119.99 a year. No trial trap. A reminder arrives 24 hours before every charge and can't be turned off. Cancelling takes two taps in the app. VIP never sees an ad.

Back after 12 days

A win-back push: “Who sent the text? 👀 E8 of The Heiress Returns is free for you today.” The offer comes once per series. The series page offers Story Rewind: E1–E6 in 20 seconds, spoiler-free.

Gift an episode

Send a Cliffhanger gives a friend E8 for free, and they need no account to start. When they finish it, the sender gets +100 bonus coins. Two gifts a week.

Ten rules that are part of the product

They answer the biggest complaints about the top short-drama apps on Google Play, and they live in the code. In the admin they show as Locked under Monetization › Fair-play rules, so no one can switch them off by accident.

  1. The price shows in coins and money before every unlock.
  2. Bonus coins are spent first; paid coins never expire.
  3. The ledger never loses a row. Refunds are new rows, and every admin correction needs a reason.
  4. No episode is charged twice; unlocks belong to the account.
  5. Price lock: a series never gets more expensive for someone already watching it.
  6. Auto-unlock is off by default and always stays inside the daily limit.
  7. One ad = one reward, 30 seconds at most, never playable. A failed ad still pays out.
  8. VIP: no trial trap, the renewal date up front, a reminder 24 hours before, two-tap cancel, no ads.
  9. Promise Guard: ad offers are checked on the server.
  10. Episodes run at least 45 seconds; finished series carry a Complete badge.

Review module

How the review module works

Review runs in three layers. Machines handle the obvious cases, people decide the rest, and every decision is logged with its reason. In the admin, this work happens in Creator videos and Moderation; Pilot Season and payout requests have their own approvals.

Layer 1
Automatic checks
Every upload

Scores for nudity, violence, copyright match, music match and spam. 96.4% of uploads are approved automatically. A copyright match of 90% or more hides the video at once and tells the creator.

Layer 2
Human review
Admin › Creator videos

Pilots, reported and flagged videos. Tabs: Needs review · Flagged · Processing · Live · Needs changes. Median decision time 38 minutes; SLA 2 hours.

Layer 3
Reports and moderation
Admin › Moderation

One queue for videos, comments, reviews and creator identity checks, with rules, SLAs, warnings, account restrictions and appeals.

The moderation queue

Three panes: the list on the left, details in the middle, the decision on the right. Each item shows how long it has waited and how many reports it has. Items near the 2-hour SLA turn amber; items past it turn red.

  • Approve · keep itKeep it. The report was wrong.
  • RemoveTake it down. The reason goes to the user.
  • Remove + warn userTake it down and warn the user.
  • Restrict account (7 days)No comments, uploads or purchases for 7 days. Anything already unlocked still plays.

Users see the reason; internal notes are for moderators only. A user can appeal once, and a different moderator handles the appeal. Keys: J and K to move, A to approve, R to remove.

Rules (the Rules tab)

  • Blocked-word lists in English, हिन्दी, Bahasa, Português and Español.
  • Links and phone numbers blocked in comments and reviews.
  • AI moderation scores toxicity, harassment and spoilers.
  • Unlabelled spoilers are blurred automatically.
  • After 20 reports an item is hidden until a moderator decides.
  • Comments from accounts under 24 hours old wait for approval.
  • At most 5 comments a minute. Review SLA: 2 hours.
  • Copyright match of 90% or more → hide and notify.

Not built yet

What isn't built yet

Worth being clear about before deciding anything. The prototypes exist to settle what to build and why; the real apps are still to be built.

  • No server, database or API.All data lives in data.js as samples. A reload resets everything.
  • No real sign-in.The Apple, Google and phone OTP buttons are for show.
  • No real payments.The Google Play sheet is a sample; no money moves.
  • No real video.The player runs on stills and subtitles at 3× speed.
  • No real ads or push.The ad is a 5-second sample; pushes are banners inside the phone.
  • No real AI.Scene Search and Story Rewind use pre-written examples.
  • Generated artwork.Posters and character art are generated; real, licensed posters are needed.
  • Admin changes don't reach the app.Only the accent colour carries across.
  • No SaaS or Partner Studio module.Outside companies registering, getting approved and connecting platforms has only been discussed; there is no code.
  • The real apps haven't started.Flutter (Android and iOS), the Laravel admin and the Next.js web are still to come. These prototypes are their blueprint; each part of data.js becomes a database table.
